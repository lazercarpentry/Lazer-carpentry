FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

Quick answers for builders, developers and project teams before sending plans or booking a site discussion.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Lazer Carpentry is fully licensed and insured. Insurance certificates and required builder documentation can be supplied on request.

What carpentry scopes do you take on?

Steel framing, timber framing, lock-up, fix-out, fit-off, doors, architraves, hardware, cladding, decks, pergolas and detailed carpentry packages.

Do you work directly for builders?

Yes. Lazer Carpentry is built for builder-led residential and commercial carpentry packages across Canberra and surrounding regions.

Can you price from plans?

Yes. Send through the plans, scope, programme and site requirements, then we can discuss availability, staging and quoting.

Do you handle full carpentry packages?

Yes. We can cover frame stage through to lock-up, internal fix-out, fit-off and cladding where the project requires a complete package.

What areas do you service?

Canberra, ACT and surrounding regions depending on the scope, programme and crew availability.
